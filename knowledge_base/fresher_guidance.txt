[Topic: Fresher Preparation - Communication & Presence]
Guidance: How should a college graduate or fresher conduct themselves in technical and behavioral interviews?
Key Points:
1. Clarify the question before answering: If an interview question or edge case is ambiguous, ask thoughtful clarifying questions rather than guessing.
2. Think aloud: In technical problem-solving, explain your thought process step-by-step. Interviewers care about the reasoning approach, not just the final syntax.
3. Admit knowledge gaps gracefully: If you do not know a specific framework API, state your foundational knowledge and explain how you would quickly research and solve it.
4. Structure responses: Use bullet points, frameworks (like STAR for behavioral questions or Definition -> Architecture -> Example for technical concepts).

[Topic: Fresher Preparation - Resume and Project Defense]
Guidance: How to effectively explain academic and personal capstone projects:
Key Points:
1. Architecture overview: Be ready to draw/describe the high-level architecture (Frontend, Backend API, Database, Third-party services).
2. Your specific contribution: Clearly identify what YOU wrote versus what third-party libraries or team members contributed.
3. Trade-offs and decisions: Be prepared to explain why you chose technology X over technology Y (e.g., SQLite vs PostgreSQL, Flask vs Express).
4. Edge cases & scalability: What happens if 10,000 users access the application simultaneously? How would you optimize queries, cache data, or handle authentication?
